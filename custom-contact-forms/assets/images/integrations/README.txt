Logos for Forms > Integrations.

Add an official logo file named after the card, and the card shows it in
place of the colored badge. SVG works best; PNG or WebP also work.

  mailchimp, mailerlite, brevo, kit, activecampaign, mailpoet,
  webhooks, akismet, hcaptcha, turnstile, recaptcha,
  stripe, paypal, twilio, places

Use each company's own logo files from its brand or press page, and follow
its brand guidelines.
